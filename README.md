## READ THIS FIRST — this is not a drop-in weapon pack

Elemental Battlegrounds Arsenal sounds like what it is. A mod that adds an insane ammount of element-themed weapons to the game. 

**For your first session, do not replace your entire loadout at once.** EB starts from your current terminal loadout for exactly this reason. (Or at least i sure hope it does) Keep the weapons you already understand, then replace them gradually with elemental weapons that fill similar roles. If you like the standard Piercer, for example, try replacing that weapon with somthing similar. The storm revolver is pretty much the same thing but still makes you feel "wow so novel". Over time you will add more weapons until maybe your entire loadout is new. Execpt for the fact that you sill have your coin revolver equiped becouse like hell your leaving that.

## How to use

1. With the mod enabled, pause the game while in a level and press the button that looks like this mods logo. PluginConfigurator is only a fallback/debug entrance and balance editor (Like you care). 
2. Pick any of the 15 positions you want to replace. Such as. Revolver / Close / Rapid / Ultimate / Explosive, and of course one of their three positions.
3. Left-click an implemented element or an explicit vanilla weapon to assign it. **Right-click an element** to read its weapon overview before committing to it. Vanilla weapons are at the bottom.
4. Use **Weapon Directory** when you need to find which element contains a familiar vanilla/GL weapon. Or just click the vanilla weapon button for the weapon you want at the bottom. No i dont know why i made weapon directory...
5. Working changes are remembered automatically and become active on the next level or on level restart. Mid-level **Apply Now** is deliberately cheat-only so a normal run cannot rebuild its arsenal room-by-room.
6. Named presets are manual snapshots. **Save New / Save Current / Rename / Load / Delete** are the only actions that modify or load a named preset; ordinary slot editing does not silently overwrite one.

Finding a strong or fun combination is part of the mod. No, you doing a full 5 fire 5 water 5 grass build is not going to garantee a coherent loadout. In fact its probably going to fry all the muscle memory in your brain.
